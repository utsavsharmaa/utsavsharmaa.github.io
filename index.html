About me

Life / Hobbies

Away from the desk I’m usually sketching, taking photographs or shooting short films. Four years of Applied Arts in Varanasi taught me to look closely at the things most people walk past.

I think good design starts with noticing something odd, and then asking why it happens.

Skills / Tools

I work across branding, packaging, campaign design, UI/UX and retail experience, with photo and film along the way. I design in Figma, and I’m learning Framer to bring interactions to life.

Whatever the format, I want the result to be clear, useful and a little surprising.

My workflow

A clear process leads to better work. Mine starts with a question and ends with something people can use.

  1. 01.

    Notice

    Every project starts with something that doesn’t add up: a craft people walk past, or a hiring process nobody can track. I write the question down before anything else.

  2. 02.

    Research

    Then I go and find out why. Surveys, interviews, field visits and a look at what already exists turn a hunch into a clear problem.

  3. 03.

    Design

    Sketches first, then Figma. A brand, a box, a set of screens or a store: I explore wide, then narrow down with feedback.

  4. 04.

    Make & test

    Prototypes, mockups and printed samples go in front of real people. I refine until it works in their hands, then hand over files ready to build or print.

I start by noticing what doesn’t work, research until I understand why, design an answer, then test it with real people.
