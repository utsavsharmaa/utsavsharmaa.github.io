OVERVIEW

A staffing platform where employers, agencies and job seekers meet

Aadmiwala came to me as a live brief: a Patna start-up wanted one place where a business could hire blue-collar staff through trusted local agencies. Over about a month I took it from research to a shipped website and dashboards for three kinds of users. This page walks through how I got there.

The client

Aadmiwala is a Patna-based staffing start-up for blue-collar roles — security, housekeeping, office support, cooks, electricians. It connects employers with verified recruiting agencies (HUBs) and the job seekers they place.

My role

End-to-end product design over about a month: research, information architecture, flows, visual design, components and prototype — a launch website (Phase 1), then a three-sided platform with a dashboard for every role (Phase 2).

What shipped

80+ screens across two phases · 3 role dashboards · 8 prototype flows · brand guidelines · a site that ran live for about a month.

PROBLEM

Everyone in the chain loses time to the same broken handoff

Next I followed a single hire from the business, to the agency, to the worker. Each side was losing time to the same handoff, just in a different way. That gave me one question to design around.

Employers

A company with sites in Patna, Delhi and Noida needs a separate agency, contract and invoice per city — and no way to compare quality before signing.

Recruiting HUBs

Good local agencies live as one-line listings on directories like IndiaMART and Justdial. They compete on price calls, not on track record.

Job seekers

Fake postings, middlemen who charge a fee, and silence after applying. Most never hear back about where their application stands.

How might we give a business one accountable vendor for staff in any city — while keeping local agencies in business and workers protected?

Sources: Deloitte India, Blue-Collar Workforce Trends 2025; WorkIndia salary report via CB Insights (Mar 2026).

CLIENT REFERENCES

Two sites the client pointed me to

Before designing anything, the client sent me two websites and said, "it's kind of what we are looking for." I studied both to understand the product they had in mind: who it serves, how it makes money, and what a visitor is asked to do.

CBREX

cbr.exchange

WHAT IT DOES

A B2B hiring platform. A company posts a role, and CBREX routes it to the right firms from a network of 4,000+ recruiting agencies, all under a single contract. The company pays only when it hires. Agencies sign up free and earn a commission per hire.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

The model: one platform between businesses and many recruiting agencies, with one contract and one invoice. It also has a separate way in for agencies (its own "Recruiting Agencies" page and sign-up), plus an FAQ and "Book a Demo" on every page.

Workmate

workmate.asia

WHAT IT DOES

A "workforce-as-a-service" platform for frontline, blue-collar work in Indonesia and Thailand. It matched pre-screened workers to nearby businesses and handled contracts, attendance and pay, cutting agencies out. The site is offline now; this is from public coverage of the company.

WHAT I TOOK FROM IT

The audience: blue-collar and frontline staff, with verification and protection for the worker. The difference: Aadmiwala keeps the local agencies and adds the worker as a third user, which is where "one job, three sides" comes from.

HOW THEY COMMUNICATE

Aadmiwala sits in the middle of every conversation

Three users meant three conversations that could easily go wrong. I decided Aadmiwala would sit in the middle of all of them, so nobody has to chase anyone on the phone and the worker is never left to negotiate alone.

Employer

Northline Logistics

Employer sees

Its jobs, HUB requests with scores, the candidate pipeline, interviews, one invoice

Aadmiwala

Platform + account manager

Aadmiwala sees

Verification, HUB scores and response times, support tickets, billing and payouts

Recruiting HUB

Kaveri Facility Solutions

HUB sees

Jobs in its cities, engagements, its candidates, placements and feedback reports

Job seeker

Ramesh Kumar

Job seeker sees

Verified jobs, applications, interview times, documents, chat with the HUB

Employers and job seekers never negotiate directly: interview requests go through the HUB, which keeps the worker protected and the HUB accountable.

REFLECTION

What I learned, and what I would design next

Looking back, the biggest lessons weren't about visuals. They were about naming things once, giving every user a real seat, and making every number answer "compared to what?"

Name each user once

The first files called the agency five different things. One name per role — Employer, Recruiting HUB, Job seeker — makes copy, flows and data line up.

Design both sides of a marketplace

Phase 1 gave agencies accounts while businesses only had forms. Phase 2 gave every role its own dashboard.

Dashboards should drive a decision

Every number now answers "compared to what?" and every list ends in an action.

Wire the prototype end to end

Most links in the shipped prototype had no destination. Wiring eight end-to-end flows exposed the missing screens.

Next

• Ops console for Aadmiwala to verify agencies and route requests • Responsive and mobile layouts for every page • Pilot the worker app in Hindi with one partner agency • WhatsApp updates for every status change • Instrument real metrics: requests, time to fill, repeat business
